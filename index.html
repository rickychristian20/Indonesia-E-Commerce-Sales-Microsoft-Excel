Business Overview

From raw transaction data to business recommendations.

Manajemen membutuhkan gambaran yang lebih jelas mengenai perubahan sales antarperiode dan dimensi bisnis yang berkontribusi terhadap perubahan tersebut.

Analisis digunakan untuk mengidentifikasi kapan perubahan terjadi, dimensi yang berkontribusi, serta pola pelanggan dan waktu yang perlu diperhatikan.

Business Questions

01 — Bagaimana performa penjualan berubah dari waktu ke waktu?

02 — Dimensi bisnis apa yang berkontribusi terhadap perubahan sales?

03 — Pola pelanggan dan waktu apa yang perlu diperhatikan?

Key Findings

Analysis is structured around the business story, not only the charts.

01 • Monthly Trend

February sales declined 16.4%

Sales moved from $116.29K in January to $97.22K in February, then recovered to $109.46K in March. The decline was therefore a period-specific drop rather than a continuous decline.

02 • Diagnostic Analysis

Transaction volume is an important signal

February transactions fell from 1,965 to 1,654. The largest change came from Normal customers, whose sales were 24% lower than January. This pattern is treated as a contributing signal, not proof of causation.

03 • Customer Segment

Members contributed more sales

Member sales reached $189.69K, compared with $133.27K for Normal customers. Average transaction value was also higher for Members, around $336 versus $306.

04 • Location

Naypyitaw as a performance benchmark

Naypyitaw recorded the highest sales at $110.57K. Yangon and Mandalay can be evaluated further based on their sales, quantity, rating, and monthly patterns rather than assuming a single cause.

Business Recommendations

Recommendations are framed as actions to test and monitor.

Test targeted promotion for Normal customers.
Run an early-February promotion and measure changes in transaction count and sales.
Use Naypyitaw as a performance benchmark.
Compare Yangon and Mandalay against the benchmark and investigate dimensions with meaningful gaps.
Evaluate the 19:00 peak period.
Use transaction patterns as a reference for staffing and operational readiness during busy hours.
